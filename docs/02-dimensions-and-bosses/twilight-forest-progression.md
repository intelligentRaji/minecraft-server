# 👑 The Twilight Forest Boss Progression

**The Twilight Forest** is a magical realm covered in perpetual twilight, ancient ruins, and challenging dungeons.

> [!WARNING] Progression Gating
> The Twilight Forest has an **environmental protection curse**. If you attempt to enter a high-tier dungeon or boss area before defeating the previous boss, you will suffer environmental debuffs (blindness, acid rain, or slowness). Follow the boss order below!

---

## 1. Creating the Portal
1. Dig a **2x2 hole** (1 block deep) in the dirt.
2. Fill the hole with **Water Sources** (infinite water pool).
3. Surround all 12 edge blocks of the pool with **Flowers, Mushrooms, or Saplings**.
4. Throw a single **Diamond** into the water pool.
5. Lightning will strike the pool and open the portal. Jump in!

---

## 2. Boss Progression Checklist

Follow this exact sequence to lift the biome curses:

### Phase 1: The Forest
* [ ] **1. Naga (Naga Courtyard):**
  * *Location:* Large stone maze courtyard in the Forest.
  * *Strategy:* Attack the armored serpent from behind; hitting its head deals reduced damage.
  * *Unlocks:* Lich Tower shield lowered.

* [ ] **2. Twilight Lich (Lich Tower):**
  * *Location:* Massive obsidian-topped tower.
  * *Strategy:* Deflect the Lich's ender pearls back at him to shatter his protective shields before you can harm him.
  * *Unlocks:* Swamp, Dark Forest, and Snow biomes.

---

### Phase 2: The Swamps & Glaciers
* [ ] **3. Minoshroom (Labyrinth):**
  * *Location:* Deep underground maze in the Swamp.
  * *Drops:* **Meef Stroganoff** (eating this is required to survive the Fire Swamp!).

* [ ] **4. Hydra (Hydra Lair):**
  * *Location:* Hollow fiery hill in the Fire Swamp.
  * *Strategy:* Aim for its open mouths when it roars to deal massive critical damage.

* [ ] **5. Knight Phantom (Goblin Knight Stronghold):**
  * *Location:* Underground fortress in the Dark Forest. Requires a Trophy on the entrance pedestal.
  * *Strategy:* Battle 6 ghost knights hovering with weapons.

* [ ] **6. Ur-Ghast (Dark Tower):**
  * *Location:* Tower reaching the sky in the Dark Forest.
  * *Strategy:* Use the **Ghast Traps** on the roof to blast the Ur-Ghast out of the sky.

---

### Phase 3: The Snowy Highlands
* [ ] **7. Alpha Yeti (Yeti Cave):**
  * *Location:* Snowy forest caverns.
  * *Strategy:* Watch out for ice boulders falling from the ceiling.

* [ ] **8. Snow Queen (Aurora Palace):**
  * *Location:* Floating glass palace atop the Glacier.
  * *Strategy:* Break her ice shield sled while dodging ice crystal minions.
