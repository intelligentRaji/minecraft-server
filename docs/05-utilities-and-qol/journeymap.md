# 🧭 JourneyMap Navigation & Web Map

**JourneyMap** provides real-time mapping of your world as you explore.

---

## 1. Primary In-Game Controls

* **`J`:** Open the Fullscreen Map.
* **`B`:** Create a new Waypoint at your feet.
* **`[` / `]`:** Zoom Minimap in / out.
* **Vertical Cave Slices:** When underground, use the vertical slider on the right side of the fullscreen map to view specific Y-levels and caves.

---

## 2. Opening the Live Map in a Browser (Second Monitor / Phone)

JourneyMap has a built-in web server that lets you view your map on a second screen or phone without opening the in-game fullscreen menu:

1. In Minecraft, press **`J`** $\rightarrow$ Click **Options** (gear icon) at the bottom.
2. Select **Web Map**.
3. Toggle **Web Map Enabled** to `ON`.
4. The default port is `8080`.
5. Open your web browser on your PC or tablet and navigate to:
   ```text
   http://localhost:8080
   ```
6. You will see a live, updating map of your world while playing!

---

## 3. Player Radar
* Other players are rendered with their **actual skin face avatars** and a small directional cone showing where they are looking.
* Hovering over any player on the fullscreen map displays their name, altitude, distance, and coordinates.
