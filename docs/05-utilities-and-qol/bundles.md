# 🎒 1.21 Bundles Guide

**Vanilla Backport** brings Minecraft 1.21.2's **Bundles of Bravery** mechanics to our Forge 1.20.1 server (with Vanilla Backport's worldgen, biome, tree, and mob-spawn features disabled in `data/config/vanillabackport-common.toml` to keep Biomes O' Plenty and Dynamic Trees conflict-free).

---

## 1. Crafting & Dyeing

* **Recipe:** `1 String` (top-center) + `1 Leather` (center) in any crafting grid.
* **Dyeing:** Combine a crafted Bundle with any of the 16 Minecraft dyes in a crafting grid to color-code your bundles.

---

## 2. Controls & Capacity

* **Capacity:** Holds up to **64 items' worth of weight** inside a single inventory slot (1 full stack of 64-stackables, 4× 16-stackables like Ender Pearls, or 1 unstackable item).
* **Insert:** Left-click or right-click items onto the bundle (or pick up the bundle and right-click items in your inventory).
* **Select & Remove:** Hover over a bundle and **scroll the mouse wheel** to highlight a specific item inside its tooltip, then **right-click** to take that item out.
* **Empty Everything:** Right-click while holding the bundle in your hand (outside an inventory) to dump its contents onto the ground.
