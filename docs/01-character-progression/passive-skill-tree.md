# 🌳 Passive Skill Tree Guide

The server uses **Passive Skill Trees** (`v0.6.14a`) to provide a complete, deep character progression system inspired by *Path of Exile*.

There are **no game-breaking superpowers** (no free flight, no instant teleportation). Instead, your character grows steadily stronger through small, meaningful percentage buffs, crafting synergies, and major keystones across a massive interconnected wheel of **588 skill nodes**.

---

## 1. Opening the Skill Tree
* Press **`O`** anywhere in-game to open the passive skill tree interface.
* Use your **scroll wheel** to zoom in and out, and **left-click + drag** to navigate across the massive wheel.

---

## 2. The Six Starting Archetypes

Every player begins by choosing their starting class node. Because the entire 588-node tree is interconnected, **you can eventually path across branches into other classes** as your character gains more points!

| Class | Focus | Core Specializations & Bonuses |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ⛏️ **Miner** | Resource Gathering | Pickaxe break speed (+15% base), underground haste, bonus ore yields, and subterranean survival. |
| 🔨 **Blacksmith** | Heavy Combat & Armor | Forged weapon damage, armor toughness, shield block chance, life-on-block, and equipment repair efficiency. |
| ✨ **Enchanter** | Magic & Experience | Chance for free enchantments, increased enchanting power, and global EXP gain multipliers. |
| 🍳 **Cook** | Nutrition & Sustain | Synergizes with [[farmers-delight\|Farmer's Delight]]: eating speed, food saturation buffs, health regen while fed, and crop duplication. |
| 🏹 **Hunter** | Ranged Combat & Agility | Bow draw speed, arrow velocity, arrow retrieval chance, critical strike chance, and mob loot duplication. |
| 🧪 **Alchemist** | Brewing & Status Effects | Potion drink speed, buff durations, brew potency, poison damage, and immunity to your own splash potions. |

---

## 3. Earning Skill Points
* **Character Experience (XP):** As you play the game, mine, cook, and slay monsters, your vanilla EXP feeds directly into earning passive skill points (up to the server cap).
* **Wisdom Scrolls:** Consuming a rare **Wisdom Scroll** grants an immediate passive skill point.

---

## 4. Resetting Your Points
If you wish to respec your build or try a completely different starting archetype:
* Use an **Amnesia Scroll** to reset your allocated skill points.
* *(Note: Amnesia scrolls consume a percentage penalty of levels upon use, so plan your builds thoughtfully).*
