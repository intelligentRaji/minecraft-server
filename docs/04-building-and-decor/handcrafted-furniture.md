# 🪑 Handcrafted Furniture & Woodworking

With **Handcrafted** and **Every Compat (Wood Good)** installed, you can build fully detailed, cozy interiors using both vanilla woods and any **Biomes O' Plenty** timber.

---

## 1. Crafting Furniture

Craft a **Hammer** (Wood or Stone) to craft furniture items at a crafting table:

* 🪑 **Chairs & Benches:** Right-click to sit down. Press `Shift` to stand up.
* 🛋️ **Couches:** Place adjacent couch blocks to form modular L-shaped sectionals.
* 🛏️ **Nightstands & Desks:** Functional wooden furniture with small storage drawers.
* 📚 **Shelves & Counters:** Display blocks for placing pottery, trophies, and crockery.

---

## 2. Cushions & Sheet Customization

* **Cushions:** Craft cushions from wool and right-click them onto chairs or couches to add padding.
* **Custom Sheets:** Right-click placed cushions with carpets or dyes to change their texture and color scheme.

---

## 3. Biomes O' Plenty Wood Compatibility

Thanks to **Every Compat**, all furniture pieces can be crafted directly from Biomes O' Plenty wood types:
* 🌲 **Fir & Pine:** Dark rustic cabins.
* 🌲 **Redwood:** Massive lodge dining tables.
* 🌸 **Jacaranda & Cherry:** Delicate pastel furniture.
* 🌿 **Willow & Mahogany:** Deep jungle and swamp aesthetics.
