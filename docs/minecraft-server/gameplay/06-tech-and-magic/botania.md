# 🌸 Botania: Floral Magic & Mana

**Botania** is a magic-tech mod powered by **Mana** harvested from mystical flowers rather than electricity or pipes. Everything is documented in-game via the **Lexica Botania** (powered by Patchouli).

---

## 1. First Steps

1. **Gather Mystical Flowers:** Collect glowing flowers around the world (16 colors) and craft them into **Mystical Petals**.
2. **Craft the Lexica Botania:** Combine `1 Book` + `1 Mystical Petal` (any color). It contains full recipes, diagrams, and progression chapters.
3. **Petal Apothecary:** Fill with a Water Bucket, drop in petals/ingredients, and finish with **Seeds** to craft functional and generating flora.
4. **Pure Daisy:** Place logs or stone around a **Pure Daisy** to transmute them into **Livingwood** and **Livingrock**—the core building blocks of Botania.

---

## 2. Generating & Moving Mana

* **Generating Flora:** Start with **Endoflames** (burn furnace fuel like Coal or Charcoal) or **Hydroangeas** (consume nearby water sources).
* **Mana Spreader & Mana Pool:** Place a **Mana Spreader** next to your generating flowers and aim it at a **Mana Pool** using the **Wand of the Forest** (`Shift + Right-click` to bind).
* **Functional Flora:** Flowers placed near a Mana Pool spend Mana to help your base (e.g., **Hopperhock** picks up items, **Agricarnation** speeds up crops, **Orechid** turns stone into ores).

---

## 3. Progression Milestones

* **Runic Altar:** Craft elemental and sin runes using Mana and Livingrock.
* **Terrestrial Agglomeration Plate:** Combine Manasteel, Mana Pearl, and Mana Diamond using half a Mana Pool to forge **Terrasteel**.
* **Portal to Alfheim:** Open an Elven trade gateway to unlock **Elementium**, **Dreamwood**, and upgraded gear.
* **Gaia Guardian:** Summon the boss on an active Beacon surrounded by Gaia Pylons for end-game relics and Dice of Fate drops.
