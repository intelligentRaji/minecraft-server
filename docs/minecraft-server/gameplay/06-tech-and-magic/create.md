# ⚙️ Create: Mechanical Power & Automation

**Create** adds kinetic machinery, contraptions, trains, and rotational automation using gears, belts, shafts, and water/steam power.

---

## 1. Getting Started

* **Wrench:** Right-click components to rotate them; **Shift + Right-click** to pick up Create blocks instantly.
* **Engineer's Goggles:** Wear them to see exact **Stress Units (SU)**, RPM, and fluid/item contents when looking at machines.
* **Ponder System:** Hover over *any* Create item in your inventory or JEI and **hold `W`** to watch an interactive 3D tutorial showing how it works.

---

## 2. Early Rotational Power

1. **Hand Crank:** Quick manual rotation for early testing or a single Millstone.
2. **Water Wheel / Large Water Wheel:** Reliable, zero-maintenance early power when placed in flowing water.
3. **Windmill Bearing:** Attach Wool or Sails to generate high Stress capacity as your base grows.
4. **Steam Engine (Blaze Burner + Boiler):** Late-game compact powerhouses.

---

## 3. Core Processing Machines

* **Millstone & Crushing Wheels:** Grind ores, grains, and stone into dusts and bonus resources.
* **Mechanical Press & Depot:** Press ingots into **Sheets** (Iron, Brass, Copper, Gold) and compact items in a **Basin**.
* **Mechanical Mixer & Basin:** Mix alloys (like **Brass** with Copper + Zinc over a heated Blaze Burner) and craft bulk recipes.
* **Encased Fan:** Blow air through **Water** (Washing), **Lava** (Smelting), **Fire** (Smoking), or **Soul Fire** (Haunting).
