# 🛡️ FCM Clans Guide

**FCM Clans** lets players create and manage their own clans, customize their clan identity, and handle diplomacy or clan wars through an in-game GUI.

---

## 1. Opening the Clan Menu

* Check **Options → Controls → Key Binds → FCM Clans → `Open Clan Menu`** to see or bind your hotkey.
* From the Clan Menu you can browse existing clans, apply to join one, or create your own.

---

## 2. Creating & Customizing a Clan

When creating a clan, the **Clan Owner (Leader)** can configure:
* **Clan Name:** 3 to 24 characters.
* **Description:** Short summary shown in the clan browser.
* **LuckPerms Tag & Clan Color:** Custom tag and color via the built-in color picker.
* **Item Icon:** Pick any in-game item/block icon to represent your clan.

---

## 3. Managing Members & Settings

* **Join Requests:** Review and accept/decline player applications under **View Requests**.
* **Roster Actions:** Owners can **Kick** members, **Transfer Ownership**, **Leave**, or **Delete** the clan.
* **Clan Settings:** Toggle **Friendly Fire** (`ON`/`OFF`) and **Clan Tags** (`ON`/`OFF`).

---

## 4. Diplomacy & Clan Wars

* **Non-Aggression Pacts & Peace Treaties:** Send and review treaties under **Requests and Treaties**.
* **Declaring War:** Starts an active clan war between two clans. Fallen clanmates enter spectator mode until revived with a **Field Medkit** (or until the war concludes).
