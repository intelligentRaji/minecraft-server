# 🔪 Create: Slice & Dice

**Create: Slice & Dice** bridges **[[create|Create]]** and **[[farmers-delight|Farmer's Delight]]**, letting you automate kitchen prep, cooking, and farming tasks with rotational power.

---

## 1. Automated Kitchen & Farming Machines

* **Slicer:** Automates [[farmers-delight|Farmer's Delight]] **Cutting Board** recipes using rotational power (slicing meats, dough, vegetables, and stripping bark).
* **Sprinkler:** Waters crops and applies **Organic Compost / Bone Meal** over an area when supplied with fluid.
* **Automated Cooking & Mixing:** Integrates Farmer's Delight **Cooking Pot** and **Rich Soil / Organic Compost** workflows into Create's **Mechanical Mixer**, **Basin**, **Belt**, and **Funnel** pipelines.

---

## 2. Checking Recipes

Use **JEI** in-game to view Slicer and Basin-compatible Farmer's Delight recipes, and **hold `W`** on Slice & Dice blocks to view **Ponder** tutorials where available.
