# 🍲 Farmer's Delight & Culinary Arts

**Farmer's Delight** overhauls Minecraft's food and farming system, making home-cooked meals significantly more rewarding than plain steak or bread.

---

## 1. Essential Tools

* 🔪 **Knives (Flint / Iron / Diamond):**
  * When killing animals with a knife, they drop extra leather, feathers, and **raw cuts of meat**.
  * Used on a **Cutting Board** to slice foods, carve pumpkins, and strip tree bark.
* 🪵 **Cutting Board:**
  * Place ingredients on it and right-click with a Knife, Shears, or Axe to process them.
* 🍳 **Skillet:**
  * Place over fire or a stove to cook food quickly, or hold in your hand to cook on campfire embers.
* 🍲 **Cooking Pot:**
  * Must be placed above a heat source (Campfire, Magma Block, Stove, or Fire).
  * Automatically combines up to 6 ingredients into hot soups, stews, and feasts. Requires a **Bowl** to retrieve servings!

---

## 2. Key Status Buffs

Eating cooked meals gives you powerful restorative effects:
* **Nourishment:** Prevents hunger and saturation drain for several minutes (you can sprint continuously without losing food bars!).
* **Comfort:** Slowly regenerates health over time, even when your hunger bar is not full.

---

## 3. Top Meal Recipes

1. **Stuffed Pumpkin:** Feeds an entire party. Right-click with a bowl to serve portions.
2. **Dumplings & Fried Rice:** Excellent compact rations for dungeon expeditions.
3. **Noodle Soup & Beef Stew:** Long-lasting Nourishment buffs for boss fights in [[twilight-forest-progression|The Twilight Forest]].
